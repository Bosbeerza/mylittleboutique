Privacy Policy

My Little Boutique is a small private business tool for managing inventory, customers, orders and finances.

What data the app uses

Business records you enter (items, customers, orders, transactions, photos) are stored on your own device and, if you sign in with Google, in a single backup file (myshop.json) in your own Google Drive.

Google permissions

The app requests the Google Drive "drive.file" permission, which lets it see and edit only the file it creates. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Sharing

We do not collect, sell, or share your data. The developer has no access to your data. Everything stays on your device and in your own Google Drive.

Deleting your data

You can delete myshop.json from your Google Drive and clear the app's data on your device at any time. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
